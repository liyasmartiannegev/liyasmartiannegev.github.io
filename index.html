Welcome to my blog!

This blog showcases my fun theoretical papers with the goal of getting my open ended Negev Desert focused experiments performed by researchers in Israel. The focus of my papers is on connecting the Negev Desert to Mars by finding similarities in their environments in order to prepare for future Mars missions. I hope to shift the focus of the space industry towards terraformation by designing theoretical ideas that would allow for a better understanding of what it would take to sustain humans on Mars while simultaneously populating the Negev Desert by developing Mars analog research stations. My papers are not theoretical frameworks, but rather theoretical concepts with an emphasis on potential implications. The experiments mentioned in the papers are open ended and rely on the expertise of the professionals who perform them, but you do not need to be an expert to read them. The purpose of LMN is purely to shine a fun light on space research!
